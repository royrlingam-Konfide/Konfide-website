📋 How to Upload Your QuickBooks P&L

For monthly/quarterly filtering, export your P&L from QuickBooks with Compare by Month: Reports → Profit and Loss → Display Columns → Months → Export → CSV.
The dashboard auto-detects month columns and enables Monthly / Quarterly / Yearly filtering.

For a yearly snapshot, use the simple 2-column format: Label,Amount  |  Grants,220104.68

Click ⬇ CSV Template above to download a pre-formatted monthly template (fill in the numbers for each month).
