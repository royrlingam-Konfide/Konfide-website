📋 How to Upload Your QuickBooks P&L

Export your P&L from QuickBooks as CSV (Reports → Profit and Loss → Export → CSV). The uploader auto-matches line item names to this dashboard. Column names do not need to match exactly — values are matched by row label.

Alternatively, use a simple 2-column CSV with headers Label and Amount:

Label,Amount
Grants,220104.68
CCAP,658812.28
Tuition Express,1796327.38
Salaries & wages,907031.24
Rent,263300.00

Click ⬇ CSV Template above to download a pre-formatted starter file.
