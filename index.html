What We Do

Four Verticals.
One Vision.

Every dollar Konfide deploys serves a deliberate purpose — building lasting value across real estate, education, and medical training in the markets we know best.

01 — Residential

Quality Housing. Sustainable Returns.

Acquiring, improving, and managing residential properties across Chicago's most promising neighborhoods — delivering consistent income and long-term appreciation.

02 — Commercial

Anchoring Communities. Building Value.

Identifying commercial opportunities that anchor neighborhoods, create economic vitality, and deliver the kind of reliable returns that hold up across market cycles.

03 — Early Education

Investing in the Next Generation.

Through Smart Beginnings Academy — 3 early childhood education centers across Illinois serving children ages 6 weeks to 12 years — we invest in the people who build tomorrow.

04 — Medical Education

Training Physicians to Practise in America.

Delphi USMLE trains American medical graduates and foreign medical graduates to obtain their U.S. medical license — built by Dr. Lingam, who walked the same path himself.

Investment Philosophy

We build where others overlook.
We hold what others sell.

01

Long-Term Vision

We measure success in decades, not quarters. Every acquisition is underwritten for durability, not velocity — because enduring wealth is never the result of urgency.

02

Community First

Every property we acquire must serve the neighborhood it occupies. We don't extract from communities — we strengthen them, because that's where real appreciation lives.

03

Disciplined Capital

We deploy capital with conviction and patience. We don't chase trends or overpay for momentum. We wait for the right asset at the right basis — then we move decisively.
